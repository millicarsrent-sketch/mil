Car Rental Booking in Baku

Book a car rental in Baku quickly and conveniently with Millicars. Our online booking system lets you choose a vehicle, specify rental dates and confirm your booking in just a few minutes. We offer a fleet of 21+ cars including economy, comfort, business, SUV and luxury class — at transparent prices from 25 AZN per day with no hidden fees.

After submitting your request, our team will confirm the booking within 24 hours by phone, WhatsApp or email. We accept cash (AZN, USD, EUR), Visa and Mastercard bank cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. A deposit of 500–1000 AZN (depending on car class) is collected at handover and fully refunded on clean return.

Flexible delivery options let you collect the rental car wherever suits you. Choose free office pick-up, airport delivery at Heydar Aliyev for free, or free hotel delivery by arrangement. We operate 24/7, so whether you land at midnight or early morning, your car will be ready and waiting.

Every booking includes basic insurance at no extra charge. You can upgrade to medium cover (+15 AZN/day) with a reduced excess, or full cover (+40 AZN/day) for complete peace of mind with zero excess. A child seat (+5 AZN/day) is also available at booking.

For long-term rentals of 31+ days we offer significantly reduced rates. Whether you need a car for a week's holiday or several months, Millicars has competitive pricing that gets better the longer you rent. Browse our full fleet to compare models and prices.

Have questions before booking? See our how it works guide, read the rental terms, or contact us directly. You can also read customer reviews from satisfied renters who chose Millicars.
