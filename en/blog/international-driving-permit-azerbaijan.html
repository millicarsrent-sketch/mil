International Driving Permit for Azerbaijan

Do you need an IDP to rent a car or drive in Azerbaijan?

One of the most common questions from visitors: do you need an International Driving Permit (IDP) to rent a car or drive in Azerbaijan? The answer depends on where your national licence was issued.

Is an IDP Legally Required?

Azerbaijan recognises driving licences from all CIS countries (Russia, Georgia, Ukraine, etc.) and EU member states without requiring an IDP. However, for drivers from other countries — including the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and most Asian and Middle Eastern states — an IDP is strongly recommended alongside your national licence.

Strictly speaking, the law does not always require an IDP, but traffic police may detain you without one if they cannot read your national licence. Having an IDP eliminates this risk entirely.

What Is an IDP?

An International Driving Permit is a translation of your driving licence into multiple languages, including Russian and French. It is not a standalone document — it must be carried together with your original national licence at all times.

How to Get an IDP

  • Apply at your national automobile association (AA, AAA, ADAC, RAC, etc.)
  • Costs are typically very low — £5–20 depending on country
  • Issued on the same day in many countries
  • Valid for 1 or 3 years depending on the issuing country
  • Get it before you travel — IDPs cannot be issued abroad
