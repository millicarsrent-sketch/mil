Car Insurance in Azerbaijan

TPL, CASCO and rental insurance — what you need explained

Car insurance is mandatory in Azerbaijan. Here is a clear breakdown of what you need, what is included in your rental and what extra cover is worth buying.

Compulsory Third-Party Liability (TPL / MTPL)

Every car on Azerbaijani roads must carry compulsory third-party liability insurance (ICBM). This covers damage or injury caused to other people and their property. At Millicars, TPL is included in every rental at no extra charge. You do not need to arrange this separately.

CASCO (Comprehensive Cover)

CASCO covers damage to the rental car itself — from accidents, theft, vandalism or natural events. At Millicars, CASCO cover is available as an add-on:

  • Basic — included (TPL only)
  • Medium — +15 AZN/day (partial CASCO, reduced excess)
  • Full — +40 AZN/day (full CASCO, zero excess)

Which Package Should You Choose?

For city driving in Baku, Basic is fine if you are a careful driver. If you plan to drive into the mountains — narrow roads, steep drops, loose gravel — we strongly recommend Medium or Full CASCO. Even experienced drivers can encounter unexpected hazards on mountain routes like the Lahij road or the track to Khinalig village.

Security Deposit

A refundable cash deposit is required at the start of every rental:

  • Classes A–F (economy to crossover) — 500 AZN
  • Classes H–I (luxury and minivan) — 1,000 AZN

The deposit is returned in full on return of the car, provided there is no new damage and no outstanding fines. With Full CASCO, the deposit return is immediate even if minor damage occurs.
