Fuel Guide Driving in Azerbaijan

Petrol types, prices and the best stations across Azerbaijan

Fuel in Azerbaijan is inexpensive by European standards and SOCAR (State Oil Company of Azerbaijan Republic) stations are everywhere. Here is what you need to know before you fill up.

Fuel Types and Prices (2026)

  • AI-92 (Regular) — approx. 0.70 AZN per litre
  • AI-95 (Premium) — approx. 0.80 AZN per litre
  • AI-98 (Super) — approx. 1.00 AZN per litre
  • Diesel — approx. 0.80 AZN per litre
  • LPG (gas) — approx. 0.40 AZN per litre (at select stations)

Prices are set by the government and rarely fluctuate dramatically. All Millicars vehicles require AI-95 — please do not use AI-92 in our cars.

Best Petrol Stations

SOCAR stations are the most reliable and widespread — you will find them on every major highway and in all towns. They accept cash (AZN) and most accept Visa/Mastercard. Other common brands include LUKOIL, Azpetrol and Aran. In rural areas, stick to SOCAR for consistent fuel quality.

Paying for Fuel

Most city stations accept cards. On mountain roads and in remote areas, carry cash. Staff at the pump will fill the car for you — self-service is available at larger SOCAR stations but attendant service is the norm.

Fuel Stops on Key Routes

  • Baku–Quba (170 km) — stations at 40 km and 120 km marks
  • Baku–Sheki (340 km) — fill up in Shamakhi (140 km); next reliable stop is Agsu
  • Baku–Lankaran (260 km) — stations every 40–60 km along M6
  • Mountain roads (Lahij, Khinalig) — fill to full before leaving the main highway

Our cars are handed over with a full tank and must be returned full. Factor in roughly 8–9 litres per 100 km for economy cars and 10–12 litres for SUVs.
