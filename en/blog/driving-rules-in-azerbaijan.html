Driving Rules in Azerbaijan

Essential rules and tips for every visitor who plans to drive in Azerbaijan

If you plan to drive in Azerbaijan, understanding the local traffic rules will make your trip safer and help you avoid fines. Here is what every visiting driver should know.

Speed Limits

  • Urban areas: 60 km/h
  • Outside built-up areas: 90 km/h
  • Dual carriageways: 110 km/h
  • Motorways: 120 km/h

Speed cameras are common on main highways, particularly on the road to the airport and the Baku ring road.

Drink Driving

The legal blood alcohol limit is 0.0% — effectively zero tolerance. Any trace of alcohol results in a fine and possible licence confiscation.

Seatbelts and Child Seats

Seatbelts are mandatory for all occupants. Children under 12 must travel in an approved child seat. Millicars provides child seats on request — mention it at booking.

Mobile Phones

Using a handheld phone while driving is prohibited. Hands-free kits are permitted.

Roundabouts

In Azerbaijan, traffic already on the roundabout has right of way — yield to vehicles coming from the left when entering.

Parking in Baku

Central Baku has a paid parking zone (marked with blue lines). Download the Baku Parking app or use the SMS payment system. Illegal parking results in clamping or towing.

Mountain Roads

Many routes outside Baku — especially to Lahij, Khinalug and Lerik — include narrow, winding roads. Drive slowly and give way to oncoming vehicles on single-track sections. An SUV is recommended for these routes.

Documents to Carry

  • Driving licence
  • Passport or national ID
  • Rental agreement (Millicars provides a printed copy)
  • Insurance certificate
