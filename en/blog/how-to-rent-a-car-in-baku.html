How to Rent a Car in Baku: Complete Guide

Everything you need to know before your first car rental in Azerbaijan

Renting a car in Baku is one of the best ways to explore Azerbaijan. Public transport is limited to the city, and taxis outside Baku can be expensive. Having your own wheels gives you the freedom to visit remote attractions and travel at your own pace.

Required Documents

To rent a car in Baku you will need:

  • Passport — original, valid for the full rental period
  • Driving licence — national licence with at least 1 year of experience
  • International driving permit — recommended for non-CIS nationals
  • Age — minimum 21 years old

At Millicars the paperwork takes just 10–15 minutes. We accept cash deposits — no credit card required.

How Much Does It Cost?

Prices depend on the vehicle class and rental period:

  • Economy (Chevrolet Spark, Suzuki Swift) — from 25 AZN/day
  • Comfort (Kia Rio, Hyundai Accent) — from 40 AZN/day
  • Standard (Toyota Corolla, Hyundai Elantra) — from 60 AZN/day
  • SUV (Kia Sportage, Hyundai Tucson) — from 90 AZN/day
  • Business (Toyota Camry) — from 80 AZN/day
  • Luxury (Mercedes E-Class) — from 140 AZN/day

With a long-term rental of 31+ days, discounts of up to 30% apply. See the full fleet on our website.

Insurance

Third-party liability (TPL) insurance is included in every rental. Full casco coverage is available as an add-on. We recommend casco if you plan to drive beyond Baku — mountain roads require extra care.

How to Pick Up Your Car

Two options:

  1. Airport pick-up — we deliver for free to the arrivals terminal
  2. Office pick-up — 45 Uzeyir Hajibekov St., Nizami district

City delivery is also free of charge. The car is handed over with a full tank and freshly cleaned.

Things to Check

  • Inspect the vehicle on handover — note any existing marks in the condition report
  • Confirm the mileage limit — at Millicars mileage is always unlimited
  • Verify the spare wheel and first-aid kit are present
  • Carry cash for fuel — not all petrol stations accept cards
  • Read up on driving rules in Azerbaijan

Summary

Renting a car in Baku is straightforward when you are prepared. Choose the right vehicle, check your documents, pick an insurance package and enjoy your journey around Azerbaijan. At Millicars we make the whole process fast and transparent.
