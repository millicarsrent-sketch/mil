Azerbaijan Behind the Wheel

Azerbaijan is a country of incredible landscape variety. A rental car gives you complete freedom: mountain villages, subtropical forests, medieval fortresses and rural markets — all on your own schedule.

Baku → Sheki
Historic Silk Road Town
Top Route
310 km
Distance
3–3.5 h
Drive time
2–3 days
Recommended

Sheki is one of Azerbaijan's most beautiful cities. The Palace of the Sheki Khans, the old bazaar, cosy mountain guesthouses and the famous Sheki pakhlava. The drive passes through the scenic foothills of the Greater Caucasus.

🚗 Recommended: Comfort class (Kia Rio, Hyundai Accent) or standard — roads are good, though the last 30 km into the mountains are winding.
  • Leave Baku in the morning to visit the palace in daylight
  • Stop in Agsu or Yevlakh along the way
  • Book your hotel in advance during peak season (May–Oct)

Lankaran is a subtropical region in the far south of Azerbaijan. The green forests of the Talysh Mountains, spice and tea markets, fish restaurants on the Caspian shore. The Hirkan National Park with its UNESCO-listed relict forests is a highlight.

🚗 Recommended: Comfort or SUV. The main highway is excellent; an SUV or crossover is better for the national park and Talysh mountain roads.
  • Best season: April–June and September–October
  • Visit the village of Lerik — home to the country's longest-living people
  • Local Lankaran black tea is worth taking home

Guba sits at the foot of the Main Caucasian Range. Nearby — Krasnaya Sloboda, the only town outside Israel populated exclusively by Mountain Jews. The Afurja waterfall and viewpoints over snow-capped peaks are unmissable.

Tips

Before You Set Off

⛽

Fuel

Petrol stations are available along all main highways. In remote mountain areas, fill up before heading in. Fuel policy is same-to-same — return with the same level.

📱

Mobile & Maps

Bakcell and Azercell have good coverage on most highways. Signal may drop in the mountains. Download offline maps in Google Maps or Maps.me before you go.

🛣️

Speed & Rules

In cities — 60 km/h, on highways — 90–110 km/h. Police use fixed speed cameras. Zero alcohol behind the wheel. Seatbelts mandatory.

🌦️

Season

Best travel months: April–June and September–October. Mountain roads to Khinalug and Lahij may be closed in winter. Summers are hot on the plains, cool in the mountains.

Road Trip in Azerbaijan by Rental Car

Renting a car is the best way to explore Azerbaijan independently. The country is small in area but astonishingly diverse: from sunny Caspian shores to snow-capped Caucasus peaks, from the subtropical forests of Lankaran to the semi-deserts of Gobustan. With a rental car you can cover the main sights in 7–10 days.

Roads in Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan's main highways — Baku–Ganja (M1), Baku–Shamakhi–Sheki (E60/M4) and Baku–Lankaran (M3) — are in excellent condition. Mountain tracks to villages (Khinalug, Lahij, Kalbajar) require a high-clearance vehicle. For most routes any car class will do.

Documents for the Trip

To drive a rental car you need: passport, driving licence and the rental agreement. Foreign licences are accepted for up to 30 days from the date of entry into Azerbaijan. We provide all necessary documents at car pick-up.
