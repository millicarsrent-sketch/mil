Millicars — Car Rental in Baku

Providing reliable car rentals in Baku and across Azerbaijan since 2017.

About Millicars

Millicars is a car rental company founded in Baku in 2017. Over more than seven years of operation we have served over 5,000 customers and built a solid reputation as a dependable partner for tourists, business travellers, and local residents alike.

Our fleet of 21+ vehicles spans every class — from compact economy city cars to prestige business sedans and spacious minivans. We continuously refresh our fleet: all cars are 2022 model year or newer.

Our Values

Transparency. We hide nothing — every price, excess, and policy is written into the agreement. No surprises, no hidden charges.

Quality. Every vehicle is fully washed, sanitised, and mechanically inspected before handover. We guarantee all cars are in roadworthy condition.

Speed. Paperwork takes no more than 15 minutes. We respect your time and aim to make the process as quick as possible.

Availability. Our support team is on call 24/7. You can reach us by phone, WhatsApp, or Telegram at any hour of the day or night.

Our Services

Beyond standard rentals, we offer: airport car delivery in Baku, long-term rentals at reduced rates, SUV rentals for travel across Azerbaijan, and child seats.
