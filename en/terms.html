Rental Agreement

Rental Terms & Conditions

Fully transparent terms with no hidden fees. Please read the key provisions before signing.

  • The vehicle is provided in full working order with all documentation.
  • The vehicle may only be used within Azerbaijan (unless otherwise agreed in writing).
  • Prohibited: driving under the influence, racing, commercial transport, towing.
  • The renter is responsible for monitoring oil, coolant, and other fluid levels.
  • Only drivers listed in the agreement may operate the vehicle.
  • Required documents: valid passport (original) and driver's licence.
  • Non-CIS foreign nationals are advised to hold an international driving permit.
  • Minimum age: 21. Minimum driving experience: 3 years.
  • Mileage is unlimited.
  • The first two drivers are included free of charge. Each additional driver: +5 AZN/day.
  • Payment methods: cash (AZN, USD, EUR), Visa / Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
  • Deposit returned when no damage or fines are present: cash — immediately after vehicle inspection; card — within 1 month.
  • Overdue balances accrue interest at 0.1% per day.
  • Fuel policy: same-to-same — return with the same fuel level as received.
  • Free cancellation if notified at least 24 hours in advance.
  • The vehicle must be returned on the date and time specified in the agreement.
  • Late return beyond 59 minutes triggers a full additional day charge.
  • Retaining the vehicle without notice may be classified as theft.
  • Return to a different location: available for an additional fee of 20–50 AZN.

Traffic Fines

All traffic fines are issued to the company. The renter is charged:

  • The fine amount as per the notice
  • +20 AZN — administrative processing fee per fine
  • +50 AZN — per penalty point

Example: fine 50 AZN + 2 points = 50 + (2×50) + 20 = 170 AZN

  1. Stop the vehicle and do not move it
  2. Call Millicars: +994 10 311 50 01
  3. Call the police (102) — a report is mandatory
  4. Photograph the scene, damage, and the other party's documents
  • Early termination at the renter's initiative: rental fees are non-refundable.
  • Disputes are resolved in the Nizami District Court of Baku.
  • The vehicle may be fitted with a GPS tracker.

More About Rental Conditions

Millicars is committed to fully transparent rental terms. We value our customers' trust and strive for complete openness at every clause of the agreement. All conditions are discussed before signing — no hidden charges, no unpleasant surprises.

To complete a rental you need a passport and driver's licence. The process takes approximately 15 minutes. Basic insurance is already included in the rate, but you may opt for an upgraded package with a reduced excess.
