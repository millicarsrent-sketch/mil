Car Rental at Baku Airport (GYD)

Heydar Aliyev International Airport (GYD) is the main gateway to Baku and Azerbaijan, located 30 km north-east of the city centre. Millicars offers convenient car rental at Baku Airport — we deliver your chosen vehicle directly to the arrivals terminal, so you can start your journey the moment you clear customs. No taxi queues, no transfers, no waiting.

We serve all flights around the clock. Whether you land at 3 am or during the peak afternoon rush, our representative will be in the arrivals hall with your car keys ready. Airport delivery is free; return pickup from GYD is also free.

How to Book a Car at Baku Airport

1. Choose your vehicle — browse our fleet of 21+ cars and pick the class that suits your trip. Comfort class and above are recommended for airport pickups — more boot space for luggage.

2. Share your flight details — provide your flight number and arrival time when booking. We monitor live flight status and adjust automatically if your flight is delayed.

3. Collect the keys — our representative will meet you in the GYD arrivals hall holding a Millicars sign. The handover takes about 15 minutes. Bring your passport and driving licence.

About Heydar Aliyev Airport (GYD)

GYD is the only commercial airport serving Baku, handling all international and domestic flights. The airport has a single terminal building with arrivals on the ground floor. Distance from the city centre is approximately 30 km; a taxi normally costs 20–30 AZN and takes 30–50 minutes depending on traffic. Renting a car at the airport avoids any taxi negotiation and gives you full freedom to explore Baku, the Absheron Peninsula, and beyond from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rent a car at Baku Airport?

Browse our fleet online, then contact us via WhatsApp or call +994 10 311 50 01. Share your flight number and arrival time — we deliver the car to the GYD arrivals terminal for free. Our representative will meet you in the arrivals hall with a Millicars sign.

How much does car rental at Baku Airport cost?

Prices start from 40 AZN/day (Economy). Standard class from 40 AZN/day, Business from 80 AZN/day, SUV from 90 AZN/day. Airport delivery is free.

Do you track my flight if it is delayed?

Yes. We monitor real-time flight status for all bookings. If your flight is delayed, we automatically adjust the delivery time — no extra charge.

Where exactly will I find your representative at GYD?

In the Arrivals hall on the ground floor of the terminal, holding a Millicars sign. If you can't find us, call +994 10 311 50 01 — we're there around the clock.

Can I return the car at Baku Airport?

Yes. Return pickup at GYD is free. Let us know your departure flight and preferred time — our driver will collect the car at the terminal.

What documents do I need?

A valid passport and driving licence. An international driving permit (IDP) is recommended for non-CIS licence holders. A refundable cash deposit of 100–400 AZN (depending on car class) is required at handover and returned on return of the vehicle.

Is a deposit required for airport car rental in Baku?

Yes, a fully refundable cash deposit is required — from 100 AZN for Economy to 400 AZN for premium SUVs. It is returned in full when you hand the car back in good condition.
