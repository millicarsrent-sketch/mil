Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting a car in Baku — age, licence, deposit, insurance, and more.

The minimum rental age is 21. Drivers must have held a valid licence for at least 3 years. Only drivers listed in the agreement may operate the vehicle.

CIS nationals can use their national driving licence. Citizens of other countries are advised to hold an international driving permit (IDP) alongside their national licence — without it, the insurer may refuse a claim in the event of an accident.

Millicars vehicles are authorised for use within Azerbaijan only. Cross-border travel requires prior written consent and a separate clause in the rental agreement. Insurance is not valid outside Azerbaijan.

Call us immediately: +994 10 311 50 01. We are available 24/7. Our team will organise roadside assistance or a replacement vehicle. Do not attempt to repair or tow the car yourself — this may result in additional charges.

Yes. The first two drivers are included free of charge. Each additional driver costs +5 AZN/day. All drivers must be present at signing with their passport and driver's licence.

The deposit depends on the vehicle class: 500 AZN for most cars; 1,000 AZN for luxury and minivan classes (Mercedes E-Class, Mercedes-Benz Vito, Hyundai Staria). We accept cash and bank cards.

Cash deposits are returned immediately after the return inspection and fine check. Card deposits are refunded within 1 month (timescale depends on your issuing bank). Any outstanding fines or damage costs are deducted.

Free cancellation applies if you notify us at least 24 hours before the rental start. Later cancellations are non-refundable. Date or vehicle changes are possible subject to availability — please contact us in advance.
