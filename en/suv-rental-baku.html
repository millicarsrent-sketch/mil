Why Rent an SUV in Baku

Azerbaijan is far more than Baku. The country is renowned for its spectacular mountain roads, ancient fortresses and picturesque regions: Sheki, Gabala, Lahij, Khinalug. Crossovers and SUVs are the ideal vehicles for these journeys — they deliver comfort on winding mountain passes and dependability on unpaved tracks.

Millicars offers five SUV and crossover models: Hyundai Creta and Hyundai Venue (crossovers, from 50 AZN/day) and Kia Sportage, Hyundai Tucson, Toyota RAV4 (full-size SUVs, from 90 AZN/day). All vehicles are 2022–2023 model year.

Popular SUV routes from Baku: Baku–Sheki (310 km), Baku–Gabala (220 km), Baku–Lahij (180 km), Baku–Shamakhi (120 km). Read more route ideas on our blog.

Car delivery to Heydar Aliyev Airport — free. Long-term rentals of 31+ days attract our lowest daily rates. Contact us for a personalised quote.
