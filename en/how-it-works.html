How Car Rental Works

Rent a car in Baku in 3 easy steps. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Browse our fleet of 21+ vehicles online. Filter by class — economy, comfort, business, SUV, or luxury. Compare prices, specs, and availability.

Fill out our online booking form or reach us via WhatsApp. Sign the rental agreement and pay — the whole process takes just 15 minutes.

What You'll Need

📄

Required Documents

  • Valid passport (original)
  • Valid driver's licence (min. 3 years' experience)
  • International driving permit (for non-CIS nationals)
  • Minimum age: 21
💳

Payment & Deposit

  • Cash (AZN, USD, EUR), Visa / Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • Full rental amount paid at pickup
  • Security deposit: 500–1,000 AZN
  • Deposit returned after a clean return inspection
🛡️

Insurance Options

  • Basic liability (OSAGO) — included free
  • Medium coverage — +15 AZN/day (reduced excess)
  • Full coverage (CASCO) — +40 AZN/day (zero excess)
🚗

Delivery Options

  • Office pickup — free
  • Airport delivery (GYD) — free
  • Hotel / address in Baku — free. Our manager will contact you via WhatsApp to arrange the exact meeting point.
  • Available 24/7, including late-night flights

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 24 hours ahead, especially during peak season (June–September). Same-day bookings are possible subject to availability.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes — extensions are possible by arrangement. Contact us at least 24 hours before your scheduled return date. Extra days are charged at the same daily rate.

What if I return the car late?

There is a 1-hour grace period. After that, an hourly fee applies. Please let us know in advance if you expect to be delayed.

Are child seats available?

Yes! A child seat can be added for +5 AZN/day. Please mention your child's age in the booking comments.

Renting a Car in Baku

Renting a car in Baku with Millicars is a straightforward, hassle-free experience. Whether you're visiting Azerbaijan for business, sightseeing, or a family holiday, having your own vehicle gives you the freedom to explore the city and the country at your own pace. Our rental process is designed to be fully transparent so you can focus on the journey rather than the logistics.

It all starts with choosing the right car. Our fleet of 21+ vehicles covers everything from budget economy hatchbacks like the Chevrolet Spark (from 25 AZN/day) to premium business sedans like the Toyota Camry (from 80 AZN/day) and capable SUVs like the Kia Sportage and Toyota RAV4 (from 90 AZN/day). If you're planning to explore Azerbaijan's mountains and countryside, we strongly recommend our SUV rental options.

Once you've picked your car, simply fill in our online booking form or contact us via WhatsApp for instant assistance. We'll confirm your reservation within 24 hours. On the day of pickup you'll need your passport and driver's licence. The paperwork takes around 15 minutes — you sign the rental agreement, pay the rental fee and security deposit, then you're free to go.

We offer flexible delivery. You can collect the car from our Baku office, or we'll bring it straight to Heydar Aliyev International Airport for free. Hotel delivery is also available on request. We operate round the clock, including late-night and early-morning arrivals.

All cars include basic insurance at no extra charge. For greater peace of mind you can add medium or full coverage when booking. We also offer child seats as an optional extra.

For long-term rentals of 31 days or more, we offer special discounted rates. See our full rental terms for complete details, or read customer reviews to hear about other travellers' experiences with Millicars.
